§ 1. Nature of FraVeRa Journey's Activity and Legal Role of Parties

1.1. Nature of FraVeRa Journey's Activity

FraVeRa Journey conducts activity consisting of mediation in the organization of tourist, health and related services, acting as an agent (intermediary) between the client and independent service providers.

Legal effect: FraVeRa Journey is not a party to contracts for the provision of main services (accommodation, treatment, procedures, transport).

Legal basis:

  • Article 3(8) of Directive (EU) 2015/2302
    "'travel agent' means a trader who acts as an intermediary in the sale of travel services."

Source: Directive (EU) 2015/2302, OJ EU L 326 of 11.12.2015, EUR-Lex

1.2. Lack of Tour Operator Status

FraVeRa Journey does not combine travel services into packages and does not offer them at an all-inclusive price.

Legal effect: FraVeRa Journey does not bear tour organizer liability within the meaning of EU law.

Legal basis:

  • Article 3(5) of Directive (EU) 2015/2302
    "'organiser' means a trader who combines and sells packages..."

Source: EUR-Lex — Directive (EU) 2015/2302

1.3. No Travel Package and No LTA

Services offered through FraVeRa Journey do not constitute a travel package or linked travel arrangements (LTA) because:

  • they are not sold as a single service,
  • they are not covered by one price,
  • contracts are concluded separately with providers.
Legal effect: Provisions on tourist guarantees, joint liability, and insolvency protection do not apply.

Legal basis:

  • Article 3(2) and (5) of Directive (EU) 2015/2302
  • Recital 11 of the Directive
    "Separate contracts concluded with different traders should not in themselves lead to their classification as a travel package."

Source: EUR-Lex — Directive (EU) 2015/2302

1.4. Nature of Legal Relationship with Client

The relationship between FraVeRa Journey and the Client is of the nature of an agency services contract, including in particular: searching for offers, coordinating reservations, transmitting information, and organizational support for the travel process.

Legal effect: FraVeRa Journey's liability is limited to due diligence of an agent.

Legal basis (NL):

  • Article 7:401 Dutch Civil Code (Burgerlijk Wetboek)
    "The contractor must exercise the care of a good contractor in the performance of his activities."

Source: Burgerlijk Wetboek, wetten.overheid.nl

1.5. Applicable Law for Assessment of Parties' Status

The assessment of the legal nature of FraVeRa Journey's activity is conducted according to the law of the Kingdom of the Netherlands and EU law.

Legal basis:

  • Rome I Regulation (EC) No 593/2008

Source: EUR-Lex — Rome I

§ 2. Conclusion of Contract and Moment of Creation of Obligations

2.1. Moment of Conclusion of Agency Contract

The contract between FraVeRa Journey and the Client is concluded at the moment of confirmation by FraVeRa Journey of acceptance of the agency commission, in particular after acceptance of the offer and terms presented to the Client.

Legal effect for Client: An obligation arises to cooperate in the performance of agency activities and to pay agency remuneration.

Legal effect for FraVeRa Journey: An obligation arises to perform mediation activities with due diligence.

Legal basis (NL):

  • Article 7:400 Dutch Civil Code — contract of mandate (opdracht)
    "The contract of mandate is the contract whereby one party undertakes towards the other party to perform activities (...)"

Source: Burgerlijk Wetboek, wetten.overheid.nl

2.2. No Conclusion of Main Contracts by FraVeRa Journey

Conclusion of the agency contract does not mean conclusion of contracts for accommodation, treatment, procedures, transport or other benefits, which are concluded directly between the Client and providers.

Legal effect: FraVeRa Journey does not become a party to main contracts and does not assume providers' obligations.

Legal basis (EU):

  • Article 3(8) of Directive (EU) 2015/2302 — intermediary

Source: EUR-Lex, Directive (EU) 2015/2302

2.3. Nature of Confirmations and Documents

Reservation confirmations, vouchers, schedules and other documents transmitted by FraVeRa Journey have an informational and organizational nature.

Legal effect: These documents do not create independent contractual obligations of FraVeRa Journey towards the Client.

Legal basis (NL):

  • Article 6:217 BW — conclusion of contract by mutual consent

Source: wetten.overheid.nl

Services offered through FraVeRa Journey do not constitute a travel package because they are not combined and sold as a single service for one price.

FraVeRa Journey's business model does not meet the criteria for linked travel arrangements (LTA), in particular due to the lack of a single sales process and lack of organizer's financial liability.
